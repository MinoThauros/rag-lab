# Harbourline Logistics — Client Data Handling Policy

Version 3.2, effective March 1, 2026. Owner: Office of the Data Steward.
Harbourline Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample material for testing.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for how Harbourline employees and contractors collect, store, share and delete data that belongs to our clients. It applies to all client data, whether it is held in our warehouse management system (WMS), in email, or on paper.

## 2. Data classification

All client data falls into one of three tiers:

- **Tier 1 — Public:** published shipping rates, service area maps. No restrictions.
- **Tier 2 — Internal:** shipment volumes, route plans, warehouse slot assignments. May be shared with Harbourline staff on a need-to-know basis.
- **Tier 3 — Restricted:** customs declarations, client contracts, pricing agreements, and any personal information about a client's customers (names, addresses, phone numbers). Access requires written approval from the Data Steward.

When in doubt, treat data as Tier 3 until the Data Steward confirms otherwise.

## 3. Storage

Tier 3 data must be stored only in the WMS or in the encrypted document vault ("Vault"). It must never be stored on personal devices, USB drives, or personal cloud accounts. Laptops used to access Tier 3 data must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## 4. Retention and deletion

- Customs declarations are kept for **7 years**, as required by the Canada Border Services Agency.
- Shipment tracking records are kept for **24 months** after delivery, then deleted automatically.
- Personal information about a client's customers is deleted **90 days** after the related shipment is delivered, unless a dispute is open.
- When a client contract ends, all of that client's Tier 2 and Tier 3 data must be returned or destroyed within **30 days**, and the Data Steward issues a certificate of destruction.

## 5. Use of AI tools

Employees may use AI tools only if they are approved by the IT Security team. As of this version, the only approved AI tool is Harbourline's internal assistant, which runs on Harbourline-controlled servers.

- Tier 1 and Tier 2 data may be entered into the internal assistant.
- Tier 3 data may be entered into the internal assistant only for contract review and customs paperwork, and only by staff who completed the "AI and Restricted Data" training in the last 12 months.
- Entering any client data into public AI services (for example consumer chatbots) is prohibited and is treated as a security incident.

## 6. Sharing with third parties

Client data may be shared with a third party (for example a customs broker or a subcontracted carrier) only if:

1. a signed data processing agreement (DPA) with that third party is on file, and
2. only the minimum data needed for the task is shared.

Tier 3 data may never be sent by regular email. Use the Vault's secure share link, which expires after 7 days.

## 7. Incidents

Any suspected loss, leak, or unauthorized access to client data must be reported to the Security Desk within **1 hour** of discovery, by phone at extension 4400 or through the incident form on the intranet. The Data Steward notifies the affected client within **72 hours** if the incident is confirmed.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be requested in writing and approved by both the Data Steward and the Chief Operating Officer. Approved exceptions are valid for a maximum of 6 months and are reviewed at the quarterly risk meeting.
